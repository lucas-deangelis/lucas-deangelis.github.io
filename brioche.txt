Brioche

Ingredients:
- 250g of flour
- 15g of fresh yeast
- 25g of sugar
- 5g of salt
- 65g of butter
- 10cl of water
- 2 eggs
- 1 egg yolk

Steps:
- Melt the butter until it's fully liquid.
- Put the flour in a bowl. Create a well in the flour, and add the yeast, the sugar and the salt. Mix.
- Add the eggs. Mix while adding the water until you get a firm dough.
- Add the melted butter and mix well until the dough is homogenous.*
- Leave the dough to rise for 1h30 or until it has more than doubled in size.
- Defalte the dough with a spatula, and put it in a loaf pan. It should fill half of it.
- Leave the dough to rise again until it fills the pan.
- "Paint" the egg yolk over the top of the dough.
- Put the future brioche in a preheated 180°C (350°F) oven.
- When the top becomes shiny, reduce the temperature to 150°C (300°F) until you can stick a knife (or chopstick) in the center of the brioche and it comes out clean. It's done!
