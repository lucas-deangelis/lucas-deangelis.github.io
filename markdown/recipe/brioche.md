# Brioche

Ingredients:

- 250g of flour
- 15g of fresh yeast
- 25g of sugar
- 5g of salt
- 65g of butter
- 10cl of water
- 2 eggs
- 1 egg yolk

Steps:

1. Melt the butter until it's fully liquid.
2. Put the flour in a bowl. Create a well in the flour, and add the yeast, the sugar and the salt. Mix.
3. Add the eggs. Mix while adding the water until you get a firm dough.
4. Add the melted butter and mix well until the dough is homogenous.*
5. Leave the dough to rise for 1h30 or until it has more than doubled in size.
6. Defalte the dough with a spatula, and put it in a loaf pan. It should fill half of it.
7. Leave the dough to rise again until it fills the pan.
8. "Paint" the egg yolk over the top of the dough.
9. Put the future brioche in a preheated 180°C (350°F) oven.
10. When the top becomes shiny, reduce the temperature to 150°C (300°F) until you can stick a knife (or chopstick) in the center of the brioche and it comes out clean. It's done!
