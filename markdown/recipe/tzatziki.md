# Tzatziki recipe

Ingredients:

- 50cl of greek yogurst
- 25cl of crème d'isigny
- 1 large cucumber/2 small cucumbers
- the juice of 1/2 a lemon
- 3 cloves of garlic
- salt
- pepper

1. To start, put the greek yogurt and crème d'Isigny into a bowl
2. Add the lemon juice, salt and pepper
3. Grate the garlic and add it in
4. Cut the cucumber(s) into thin slices, then chop those slices until everything is pretty small
5. Add the chopped cucumber(s) into the mix
6. Salt and pepper to taste
